# About Zhaotutu

Zhaotutu is an independent AIGC creator and software developer focused on practical tools for image generation, video generation, dataset preparation, LoRA training, and creator workflows.

The public Tutu product line includes tools for model training, image and video captioning, video publishing workflows, prompt expansion, and related AIGC productivity tasks.
